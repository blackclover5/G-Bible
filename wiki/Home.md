# G-Bible Wiki

G-Bible is a free, open-source Bible study app that runs on your computer. Read translations alongside commentaries and reference works, search your installed library, and keep notes and study materials locally.

## Start here

- [[Installation and First Launch]] — run G-Bible and install your first resources.
- [[Using the Workspace]] — read multiple resources together.
- [[Search]] — search Bible text, books, morphology, topics, and personal documents.
- [[Study Tools]] — guides, maps, notes, reading plans, sermon tools, and more.
- [[Library and Resource Store]] — install, import, organize, and attribute resources.

## Personal data and optional features

- [[Privacy and Data]] — where your library and personal study data live.
- [[Backup and Synchronization]] — make backups and optionally move study data between devices.
- [[AI Assistant]] — setup, provider choices, data sent, and limitations.
