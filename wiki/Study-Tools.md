# Study Tools

G-Bible includes tools for reading, research, personal organization, and preparation. Availability and results depend on the resources installed in your library.

## Guides and exploration

The **Guides** area brings together passage and topic research, outlines, cross-references, timelines, harmonies, genealogies, and related places. A passage guide can provide context from installed resources. Topic tools draw on indexed reference data and installed books.

## Study tools menu

- **Concordance** — inspect word occurrences in the indexed library.
- **Biblical maps** — view indexed places and map resources. Map tiles may be fetched from Esri when the map is displayed; attribution appears in the app.
- **New Testament use of the Old Testament** — explore indexed quotation and allusion relationships.
- **Word lists** and **Verse memorization** — build vocabulary and memorization practice from selected material.
- **Text comparison** — compare available Bible translations.
- **Visual filters** — control visual display of tagged text.
- **Bibliography**, **Books search**, **Search synopsis**, and **Insights** — organize or expand research.
- **Copy Bible verses** and **Visual copy** — prepare selected text for copying or presentation.

## My study menu

- **Notes & highlights** — review personal annotations.
- **Favorites** — keep frequently used resources.
- **Canvas** — arrange study material visually.
- **Organise & collections** — create collections and organize modules.
- **Clippings** — collect excerpts for later reference.
- **Study workflows** — guide a repeated study process.
- **Sermon manager** and **Sermon documents** — organize preparation material.
- **Reading plans** — follow a plan and record progress.
- **Devotional creator** — assemble devotional material.
- **Prayer lists** — maintain a personal prayer list.

## Personal data

These tools save data locally in G-Bible’s database. Some records are included in `.gbz` backup/sync; attached media and installed resource files are not. See [[Privacy and Data]] and [[Backup and Synchronization]].
