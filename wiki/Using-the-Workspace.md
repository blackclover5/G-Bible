# Using the Workspace

The Workspace is a reading area made of panes (tiles). Open a Bible, commentary, dictionary, lexicon, map, or other installed resource in a pane. Each pane keeps its own place.

## Open and navigate

Choose **Open Bible…** or open a resource from **My library**. Select a book, chapter, or reference. Follow reference links within a resource or use the passage controls to move through the text.

## Compare resources

Right-click a pane and choose **Split this pane** to add a pane below or beside it. Resize panes by dragging the divider. A common arrangement is a Bible beside a commentary and a lexicon.

To move a resource into another pane, use any of these:
- the **→ move to…** dropdown in the pane's toolbar,
- right-click the tab and choose **Move to pane**,
- drag the tab onto another tab, or anywhere into another pane (including an empty one).

When the pane you move out of becomes empty, the layout collapses automatically: a two-pane layout with one pane closed becomes a single pane. Closing a pane does the same. To get two panes back, right-click a tab and choose **Add another pane beside**.

Link panes when you want them to follow the same passage. Link sets let you choose which panes move together; use **Clear links** to unlink all panes. Links do not change the underlying resources.

## Layouts and pane controls

- **Layouts** saves and restores pane arrangements.
- **Passage pane** opens passage-guide material in the workspace.
- **Word Study pane** opens a word study alongside the reading.
- **Fullscreen** expands the first pane for focused reading.
- **Reopen last** restores the most recently closed resource.
- **Close all panes** collapses the layout to one empty pane, ready for a new resource.

## Notes, highlights, and selections

Select text to access available actions such as highlighting, copying, or asking the Assistant about the selection. Notes attach to the verse/reference rather than a particular translation, so they remain useful when you switch Bible modules. Highlight appearance can depend on the source text markup; Words of Christ are colored only where the module explicitly marks those spans.

## Tips

- Keep a Bible pane and a commentary pane linked for passage-by-passage reading.
- Open a Strong’s number or lemma from tagged Bible text to inspect lexical information.
- Use **Search** for library-wide queries; browser find only searches the text currently loaded in the page.
